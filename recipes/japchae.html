Japchae

Japchae is a traditional Korean dish made from sweet potato starch noodles, stir-fried with a variety of vegetables and often served with beef. It is known for its unique texture and savory-sweet flavor, making it a popular dish for special occasions and celebrations.

Ingredients
  • 200g sweet potato starch noodles (dangmyeon)
  • 100g beef, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps
  1. Cook the sweet potato noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large pan, heat sesame oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and sauté until fragrant.
  3. Add the beef and cook until browned.
  4. Add the carrot, bell pepper, and onion. Stir-fry until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
  5. Add the cooked noodles to the pan and stir in the soy sauce and sugar. Mix well to combine all ingredients.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Sprinkle with sesame seeds before serving.
